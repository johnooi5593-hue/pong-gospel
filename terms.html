Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. What this is

Agents HQ is a private tool used only by the Pong+Gospel team to prepare our own short faith videos and send them to Pong+Gospel's own social media accounts, including TikTok. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside the Pong+Gospel team can sign up or use it.

2. How it works with TikTok

  • The Pong+Gospel team signs in once with the Pong+Gospel TikTok account, using TikTok's own login, to connect it to Agents HQ.
  • When the team approves a finished video, Agents HQ uploads it to the Pong+Gospel TikTok account's inbox as a draft.
  • The team then opens TikTok, adds the caption and sound, reviews the video and posts it themselves. Agents HQ never posts publicly on its own.
