Privacy Policy: Agents HQ for Pong+Gospel

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Who we are

Agents HQ is a private tool used only by the Pong+Gospel team to send our own videos to Pong+Gospel's own social media accounts, including TikTok. It is not offered to the public.

2. What we collect from TikTok

When the Pong+Gospel team connects its TikTok account, we receive only:

  • an access token and refresh token, so Agents HQ can upload drafts to that account;
  • the account's ID and display name (from the user.info.basic permission), so we can see which account is connected.

We do not collect information about any other TikTok users, and we do not read followers, messages, comments or viewing data.

3. How we use it

Only to upload Pong+Gospel's own approved videos to the Pong+Gospel TikTok account as drafts, and to show which account is connected. Nothing else.

4. Where it is kept

The tokens are stored only on the Pong+Gospel team's own computer, in a private file that is never shared, published or uploaded anywhere. We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone.

5. How long we keep it

Until the TikTok account is disconnected. Disconnecting in Agents HQ deletes the stored tokens. You can also remove access at any time in TikTok: Settings → Security and permissions → Apps and websites.

6. Children

Agents HQ is not meant for children and does not collect data from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
